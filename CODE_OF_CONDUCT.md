# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and evidence. Help others reproduce and understand problems.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and publication of private information are unacceptable. Do not post credentials or customer data.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock conversations, or restrict participation. They should explain decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using a contact they list publicly. If no private contact is available, request one without naming affected people or disclosing incident details. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
